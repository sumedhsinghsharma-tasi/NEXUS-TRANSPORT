E-way bills

Validity can only be extended from 8 hours before expiry to 8 hours after. Miss it and it is ₹10,000 or the tax evaded, whichever is higher — and the truck can be held.

EWBVehicleToExpiresLeft

Typing these by hand defeats the point. The EWB number and its expiry are printed on the invoice handed over at loading — photograph that and every bill tracks itself. Extending still happens on the government portal — this gets you there with the number copied and the answers ready. Doing it inside the app needs licensed API access, which is a later build.
